## 🌍 Project Overview
**EcoVision-AI** is an exhibition-ready, zero-cost, interactive climate intelligence platform engineered to simulate empirical climate risks, multi-decadal trends, and near-term projections (**2027, 2030, 2035**, and CMIP6 SSP scenarios).

### Core Modules
1. **Cinematic 3D Landing Page** — Interactive WebGL/Three.js Earth globe.
2. **Interactive Pakistan & Sindh Map** — High-resolution district-level telemetry (Karachi, Larkana, Sukkur, etc.).
3. **Climate Intelligence Dashboard** — Plotly-driven empirical climate metrics & anomaly tracking.
4. **3D Future Earth Simulator** — Digital twin simulation of heatwaves, floods, drought, and urban resilience.
5. **Change the Future** — Transparent educational decision simulator and resilience scoring engine.
6. **ClimateGPT** — Local retrieval-based climate assistant in English and Urdu.
7. **Climate Challenge** — Interactive "Climate President" resource allocation game.
8. **About & Admin Status** — System telemetry, dataset attribution, and offline exhibition controls.
