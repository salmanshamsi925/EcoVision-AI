---

### 🚨 Block 1: The Urgent Climate Challenge (15% - 30%)
*Highlighting empirical severity to hook the evaluator:*
- **Headline:** **Pakistan's Climate Reality in Numbers**
- **3 Visual Metric Badges:**
  1. **`+0.35°C / Decade`** — Empirically measured warming rate across Upper Sindh since 1980.
  2. **`52.7°C Peak`** — Record summer extreme recorded at Jacobabad/Khairpur observatories.
  3. **`+480% Anomaly`** — Catastrophic 2022 monsoon volume causing $30B+ in economic losses.
- **Problem Statement:** Existing tools present complex, fragmented raw statistics. Decision-makers need zero-friction, interactive, real-time spatial simulation.

---

### 🧠 Block 2: 4-Step Intuitive HCI Workflow (30% - 50%)
*Showcases user journey and clean interaction design:*
- **Step 1: 3D Planetary Twin (`/`)**
  *Real-time WebGL Earth globe with atmospheric shaders, cloud dynamics, and instant search across Pakistani cities.*
- **Step 2: Interactive Pakistan & Sindh GIS (`/pakistan`)**
  *Keyless dark vector GIS map with 14 prioritized Sindh district boundaries, multi-hazard telemetry (Heat, Flood, Drought).*
- **Step 3: Machine Learning & Climate Intel (`/intelligence`)**
  *Scikit-Learn linear/polynomial regression, statistical anomaly isolation ($Z > 1.75\sigma$), and 6-axis vulnerability radar chart.*
- **Step 4: Policy Simulation & Resilience Lab (`/change`)**
  *Interactive environmental sandbox testing 6 policy levers (Solar, Afforestation, Canal Lining, Mangroves) with dynamic Resilience Index score.*

---

### 🔬 Block 3: Scientific Engine & Data Provenance (50% - 70%)
*Technical credibility for evaluators:*
- **Data Provenance:** NASA POWER Agroclimatology (0.5° grid) + Pakistan Meteorological Dept (PMD 1980–2025) + World Bank GFDRR.
- **Machine Learning Core:**
  - Python `scikit-learn` Linear Regression ($R^2 = 0.784, \text{RMSE} = \pm 0.31^\circ\text{C}$).
  - $K$-Means Clustering ($k=3$) classifying districts into Hyper-Thermal, Deltaic, and Arid agro-belts.
- **Future Forecasting:** IPCC CMIP6 AR6 Shared Socioeconomic Pathways (SSP1-2.6, SSP2-4.5, SSP5-8.5) downscaled to 2027, 2030, and 2035.

---

### 🛡️ Block 4: Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) Highlights (70% - 85%)
- **Hick's Law Optimization:** Minimized cognitive fatigue by eliminating cluttered 3D gaming widgets, leaving 4 focused, linear steps.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** High-level executive KPIs upfront; deep statistical formulas accessible on-demand.
- **Inclusive Accessibility:** Cyber-Eco dark UI (`#07111F`) with color-blind compliant categorical warning accents.
- **1-Click PDF Report:** Generates an official, publication-ready Climate Action Dossier in seconds.
